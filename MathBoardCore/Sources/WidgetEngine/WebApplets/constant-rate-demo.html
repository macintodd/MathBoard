How Do Constant-Rate Problems Work?

$3.00 + $0.501 ounce · m
m = 0 ounces
Total = $3.00
Every amber $0.50 chip is one more ounce. m counts how many $0.50 chips get added to the $3.00 base.
$15.00 + $8.001 month · m
m = 0 months
Total = $15.00
Every amber $8 chip is one more month. m counts how many $8 chips get added to the $15 signup fee.
